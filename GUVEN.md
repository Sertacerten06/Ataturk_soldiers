> Site 1 [Durham Astronomy Research Cluster](https://astro.dur.ac.uk/~pdraper/gaia/gaia.htx/blink.html)
- Blink toolbox. Uses atleast 2 images to compare the changes. (which what a blink comparator does.. duh) Has color choices ,image moving capability, speed factor and if you images have WCS comp. or the pixels coordinates are aligned by NDF, its sense of shifts or rotations through 90-180-270. When you first open the toolbox, it will attempt to automacially align, but you can do it by hand on the options menu. The only problem is, it doesnt pull images from a dataset, you need to upload to it manually.
